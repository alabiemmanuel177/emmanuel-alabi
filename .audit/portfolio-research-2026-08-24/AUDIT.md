# Portfolio Research Review — 2026-08-24

## Scope

Published research index and the linked Research Programme 2026–2027 on
emmanuelalabi.com. Review covers research communication, visual hierarchy, UX,
and screenshot-observable accessibility.

## Overall verdict

Credible, unusually honest, and visually disciplined. The programme explains a
sound research philosophy, but the section currently proves intent more strongly
than momentum. Its highest-impact improvement is to surface the completed driver
drowsiness reproduction study and make the programme's next milestones concrete.

## Steps

### 1. Research index — Healthy, but evidence-light

Screenshot: `01-research-index.png`

- Strong: clear hierarchy, restrained presentation, and an explicit distinction
  between interests and expertise.
- Risk: five broad focus areas compete for attention without a visible active
  experiment, artifact, or recent update.
- Opportunity: add a compact "Current evidence" or "Latest research artifact"
  section linking the completed driver-drowsiness reproduction study.

### 2. Research programme — Strong framing, needs operational detail

Screenshot: `02-research-programme.png`

- Strong: the research question, status, dates, contents, and methodology make
  the page trustworthy and easy to scan.
- Strong: the commitments to leakage-safe reproduction, fixed seeds, negative
  results, and traceability are academically credible.
- Risk: the headline question remains a meta-question about finding a question;
  readers cannot yet see a bounded sub-question, literature gap, baseline, or
  decision date.
- Opportunity: add a "Next 90 days" block with 2–3 milestones, success criteria,
  and links to completed artifacts.

## Highest-impact changes

1. Promote the completed driver-drowsiness reproduction study into the Research
   section as a completed investigation, or explicitly cross-link it as evidence
   produced by the programme.
2. Replace some broad field labels with one current working hypothesis or narrow
   problem statement as soon as it is defensible.
3. Add dates and measurable exit criteria to the programme's first phase.
4. Add a small "Last updated" signal so repeat visitors can see momentum.
5. Keep the honesty and conservative status language; it is the section's main
   credibility advantage.

## Accessibility notes and limits

- Confirmed from the rendered DOM/source: skip link, semantic landmarks, ordered
  heading hierarchy, breadcrumb and contents navigation, visible focus treatment,
  and reduced-motion handling.
- Muted text is readable in the captured dark theme, but contrast was not
  measured across every token/state.
- Keyboard order, screen-reader announcements, zoom reflow, and mobile layout were
  not fully exercised; this is not a WCAG compliance claim.
