\cvsection{Research \& Technical Experience}

\cventry{Risk-Calibrated Semantic Navigation Under Distribution Shift}{2026}
{\small Preregistered ROS 2 / Gazebo benchmark.
\href{https://github.com/alabiemmanuel177/risk-calibrated-semantic-navigation}{github.com/alabiemmanuel177/risk-calibrated-semantic-navigation}}
\begin{cvitems}
  \item Designed and executed a preregistered simulation study testing whether
        calibrated semantic perception uncertainty, converted into Nav2
        navigation cost, improves closed-loop robot outcomes under controlled
        distribution shift; four systems share identical maps, routes, planner
        and controller families, varying only the treatment component.
  \item Hash-locked the protocol, manifest, models, thresholds, exclusions and
        analysis code before protected execution; the confirmatory campaign
        produced 6,840 unique schema-valid episodes, 6,820 entering analysis.
  \item Calibration (H1) and clean-route efficiency noninferiority (H3) passed;
        the preregistered collision-reduction (H2) and severity-interaction (H4)
        hypotheses did not. Better component calibration did not establish safer
        navigation --- reported as the result, with integrity deviations and
        limitations disclosed.
  \item Independent clean-clone reproduction regenerated all hypothesis JSON and
        key tables byte-for-byte (249 tests).
        \href{https://www.emmanuelalabi.com/research/risk-calibrated-semantic-navigation}{emmanuelalabi.com/research/risk-calibrated-semantic-navigation}
\end{cvitems}

%% ------------------------ Independent research & reproduction studies ------
\cvsection{Independent Research \& Reproduction Studies}

\cventry{Driver Drowsiness Detection --- Reproduction \& Evaluation Study}{2026}
{\small Independent computer vision study.
\href{https://github.com/alabiemmanuel177/driver-drowsiness-reproduction}{github.com/alabiemmanuel177/driver-drowsiness-reproduction}}
\begin{cvitems}
  \item Rebuilt and evaluated a classical eye-state and temporal-alerting
        pipeline under a subject-disjoint protocol on the MRL Eye Dataset
        (84,898 images, 37 subjects), training a lightweight CNN from scratch
        against a HOG+SVM baseline and measuring frame- and event-level
        behaviour.
  \item Designed an oracle-classifier decomposition showing that once
        classification accuracy was sufficient, the historical temporal
        threshold --- not residual classification error --- remained the
        dominant source of false alerts under a simulated-sequence protocol.
  \item Built a fully reproducible pipeline: deterministic splits, automated
        leakage checks, three-seed ablations, generated result artifacts,
        structured failure analysis, and 145 automated tests.
  \item \href{https://www.emmanuelalabi.com/projects/driver-drowsiness-reproduction}{emmanuelalabi.com/projects/driver-drowsiness-reproduction}
\end{cvitems}
