# Design-partner playbook

## Outreach message

Subject: How are you proving Microsoft 365 offboarding is complete?

Hi {{first_name}},

I am researching how Microsoft-focused MSPs offboard users across client
tenants. I am especially interested in the gap between “the ticket was closed”
and “every session, group, license, mailbox, and device action was verified.”

Could I spend 25 minutes walking through one recent offboarding with you? This
is a research conversation, not a product demo. In return, I will share an
anonymized summary of the workflows and failure patterns we find.

If the problem is real for your team, I may invite you to a narrowly scoped
design-partner pilot.

— Emmanuel

## Interview guide

Ask for facts and artifacts before opinions.

1. Tell me about the last employee offboarding your team completed.
2. What initiated it, and how much notice did you receive?
3. Please walk through every system and human handoff.
4. Which actions were automated, scripted, or manual?
5. How did the technician confirm each action succeeded?
6. What happens when Microsoft Graph, a tenant, or another service returns an error?
7. In the last 90 days, what was missed, delayed, retried, or impossible to prove?
8. How are mailbox ownership, OneDrive data, licenses, groups, privileged roles,
   sessions, and managed devices handled?
9. What evidence does the client receive? What evidence would an auditor request?
10. Which PSA, identity, security, and automation products are already involved?
11. Show me how you use Microsoft 365 Lighthouse today. Which offboarding steps,
    verification, reporting, or integrations still require work outside it?
12. Who owns the risk and budget for improving this process?
13. What would prevent you from granting a product narrowly scoped tenant access?
14. If a tool reliably verified this workflow, what would a pilot need to prove?
15. Would you provide a redacted checklist or test tenant for a design review?
16. If the pilot met agreed targets, what could you pay and who would sign?

Do not ask “Would you use this?” Treat compliments and feature requests as weak
evidence unless accompanied by access, artifacts, time, or money.

## Design-partner offer

### Six-week paid pilot

- Scope: one MSP, up to three test/client tenants, Microsoft 365 and Entra only
- Workflow: approved user offboarding with evidence and exception handling
- Deployment: sandbox first; production only after security and permission review
- Support: weekly 30-minute workflow review
- Pilot fee: propose USD 1,500, creditable against the first annual contract
- Founding price: locked for 24 months if the partner converts within 30 days

### Pilot success criteria

- 100% of required workflow steps produce a verified result or explicit exception
- No unauthorized production action
- At least 95% successful completion without technician re-entry
- Median technician handling time under five minutes
- Evidence report accepted by the partner's service/security lead
- Partner confirms a production price and conversion decision by pilot end

## Short-form design-partner agreement / LOI

This non-binding letter records the intent of **{{MSP}}** and **OmniOps** to
evaluate an offboarding-assurance pilot.

1. **Problem:** {{MSP}} manages Microsoft 365/Entra client tenants and wishes to
   reduce manual offboarding work while improving verification and audit evidence.
2. **Pilot:** Subject to security review, the parties intend to test OmniOps for
   six weeks across no more than three approved tenants.
3. **Access:** No production access is granted by this letter. Permissions,
   tenant consent, data processing, and rollback procedures require separate
   written approval.
4. **Participation:** {{MSP}} will provide one workflow owner, redacted process
   artifacts, a test environment where practicable, and weekly feedback.
5. **Commercial signal:** The proposed pilot fee is **USD 1,500**, creditable
   against a subsequent annual contract. Final pricing and procurement remain
   subject to agreement.
6. **Success:** The parties will evaluate verified completion, unauthorized
   action count, technician handling time, exception rate, and evidence quality.
7. **Confidentiality and data protection:** A separate NDA/DPA will be completed
   before confidential information or personal data is exchanged.
8. **Non-binding status:** Except for any separately executed NDA, DPA, or pilot
   order, this letter does not obligate either party to purchase or deliver a product.

## Outreach operating cadence

- Build a list of 60 qualified MSPs.
- Send 15 highly personalized requests per week.
- Ask every completed interview for two peer introductions.
- Review the scorecard after every five interviews.
- Stop outreach claims that produce replies but not qualified interviews.
- Do not claim that the product, integrations, certifications, or customer
  results exist before they do.
